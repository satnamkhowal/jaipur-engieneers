What is CCNA?

The Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) is an information technology (IT) certification offered by Cisco, a networking hardware developer. The CCNA is meant to verify your understanding of core networking principles that are frequently demanded in networking roles in IT.

What is Entry-Level CCNA Certification?

To become CCNA certified, you must pass Cisco's 200-301 CCNA test. There are no test prerequisites, although according to Cisco, CCNA applicants should have the following experience before taking the exam:

  • One year of experience in using and deploying Cisco products and services is required.
  • Basic knowledge of Internet protocol
  • Knowledge of networking

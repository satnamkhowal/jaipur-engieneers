About Wipro<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

Wipro Limited, headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, is one of India’s top six software companies. Wipro operates in more than 60 countries and serves clients in industries such as financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, and telecommunications. The company serves customers in 167 countries and offers services including cloud computing, artificial intelligence, robotics, computer security, digital transformation, data analytics, and other business solutions. Joining Wipro is a dream for millions of young Indians. Let\u2019s take a closer look at their talent hunt exams, through which they evaluate fresh graduates for various positions.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

What is The Wipro NLTH Test?<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

Wipro\u2019s Elite National Talent Hunt (NTH) is the fresher\u2019s hiring program to attract the best engineering graduates nationwide. Wipro enables equal employment opportunities for India\u2019s most deserving engineering talent. Wipro conducts this talent exam annually.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

Eligibility Criteria<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n
    \r\n \t
  • B.E.\/B. Tech (Compulsory degree)\/ M.E.\/M. Tech (5-year integrated course) full-time course recognised by the Central\/State Government of India<\/li>\r\n \t
  • All branches of engineering except Fashion Technology, Textile Engineering, Agriculture and Food technology<\/li>\r\n \t
  • The Wipro CGPA Criteria needs at least 60% or 6.0 CGPA in all semesters as per the university guidelines<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Students who do only full-time courses are considered for writing the exam. If you are doing part-time or correspondence or distance learning education in your degree, 10th or 12th, you are not eligible to take the test.<\/li>\r\n \t

  • One backlog is allowed at the time of the Assessment Stage.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Maximum 3 years of GAP in education allowed (10th to graduation)<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Candidates who have participated in any selection process held by Wipro in the last six months are not eligible.<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

    Evaluation Process<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

    The process of recruitment has several stages. It starts with students\u2019 registration for the NTH exam. Once registered, students can take part in the online assessment, which is the NTH test. After the candidate clears the exam, he\/she will be called for business discussion (HR round). This leads to the final stage of providing the offer letter.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

    Registration Process<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

    If you want to register for the Wipro NLTH exam, you can do so through the official Wipro careers website during the application period. You are required to fill out an online application form with all mentioned personal details and academic background.<\/p>\r\n

    Registration \u2013> Online assessment \u2013> Business discussion \u2013> LOI -> Offer letter<\/strong><\/p>\r\n

    Wipro Interview Process<\/strong><\/p>\r\n

    The Wipro interview process<\/a> consists of three stages: the NTH test, a technical interview, and finally an HR interview. Once you pass the NTH exam, you will be assessed on your technical skills and knowledge during the technical round. Lastly, you will meet with HR, where you will provide details about your personal background, educational interests, and other general information.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

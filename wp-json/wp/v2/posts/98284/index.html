                  TCS NQT Registration Process: How Can You Apply For The Exam?<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

                  TCS Interview Process<\/a> essentially starts with candidates\u2019 registration for the eligibility exam. The registration process for TCS NQT has only two simple steps. They are:<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                  Step 1 – Create an account for yourself and register via TCS iON<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                  Get started with the TCS iON Portal by visiting their official website and create a new account if you don\u2019t have one. If you already have an account (an existing user), you can sign in with your credentials. In the registration process, register as a learner and provide all necessary details, like your name, email ID, mobile number, and password in the appropriate spaces given. If you are less than 18 years of age, select the \u201cminor\u201d option and provide information about your guardian. Lastly, accept the terms and conditions and then click \u201cCreate an account\u201d. You have now successfully completed your registration.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                  Step 2: Purchase The Variants Of TCS NQT<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                  The NQT cognitive skill test is very important if you want to participate in various NQT variants, like Industry NQT and Subject NQT. The NQT bundle pack is an all-inclusive pack and you don’t need to purchase a separate cognitive skills pack. To purchase the NQT cognitive skills pack, you can log into the TCS iON home page, click on “Apply Now,” and then select the cognitive skills option. You can opt for “Buy Now” or “Activate” in case you have an activation key received via email.<\/p>\r\n

                  New users should create an account, while existing users can log in with their credentials. Provide all personal information and complete the payment process. You will receive an activation key. Activate your pack by entering the key in the given pop-up window. After confirmation, you will be redirected to “My Dashboard,” a confirmation message will be sent to your registered email or mobile number. By doing this, you can efficiently register and purchase the TCS NQT products for your exam preparation. TCS NQT passing marks are explained on the official website of TCS iON.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                  What are TCS NQT Variants?\u00a0<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

                  NQT full form is the National Qualifier Test. There are three variants in the TCS NQT exams. They are explained below.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                    \r\n \t
                  • NQT -> NQT (Cognitive Test) -> Attitudinal Alignment (Psychometric Test):<\/strong> The first variant is common for the rest of all tests. The NQT evaluates cognitive skills, including verbal, numerical, and reasoning abilities of the candidates. Then comes the Attitudinal Alignment (Psychometric Test), to assess your personality traits, work styles, and workplace dispositions.<\/li>\r\n \t
                  • Subject NQT -> IT Programming -> Basic Computer Skills -> Accountant:<\/strong> This variant checks your skills on specific job roles and skills. This test covers topics like pseudocode, programming constructs, algorithms, and the concepts of basic Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). You are expected to demonstrate your programming skills in C, C++, and Java.<\/li>\r\n \t
                  • Industry NQT -> Information Technology -> Banking -> Retail -> Banking and Financial Services:<\/strong> The third variant evaluates your expertise of industry-specific dimensions. It opens a lot of opportunities for you to begin your career with. It includes Information Technology, Retail and Banking and other Financial Services.<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

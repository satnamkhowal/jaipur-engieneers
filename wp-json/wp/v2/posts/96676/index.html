In the world of machine learning, data is king. The more data you have, the better you can train your models to make accurate predictions. But what happens when your data is riddled with missing values and inconsistencies? Missing data can be a real challenge for machine learning practitioners, but fear not! This blog equips you with an arsenal of techniques to address and in handling missing data and get your models back on track.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

What is a Missing Value?<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

A missing value, also known as missing data, represents an absent data point within a dataset. These absences can arise for various reasons, including human error during data collection, equipment malfunctions, or sensor failures.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

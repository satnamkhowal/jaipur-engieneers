                    SAP Job Openings<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

                    Associate Software Developer<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                    As an Associate Software Developer in SAP, you will have to contribute in every aspect, including coding, testing, design, and quality assurance of new features and functionalities within SAP applications. This includes developing using languages like ABAP, Java, or Angular, working within agile teams, and taking part in the entire software development lifecycle, starting from concept to deployment. The SAP package for freshers falls in the range of \u20b94.5 lakhs to \u20b97.5 lakhs per year.<\/p>\r\n

                    Junior (Functional\/Technical) Consultant<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                    Junior consultants assist in implementing and optimising SAP solutions for clients. This involves gathering business requirements, configuring SAP modules (e.g., FI\/CO, MM, SD), conducting testing, providing user training, and troubleshooting basic issues. They work under the guidance of senior consultants to deliver effective solutions. The SAP fresher salary falls between \u20b93.5 lakhs and \u20b96.5 lakhs per year for this role.<\/p>\r\n

                    Associate Business Analyst<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                    An associate business analyst bridges the gap between business needs and SAP system capabilities. They analyse business processes, assist in gathering detailed requirements from stakeholders, document specifications, and support the configuration and testing of SAP solutions to ensure they align with business objectives. The SAP salary for freshers in Bangalore ranges from \u20b94 L to \u20b97 L per year for this role.<\/p>\r\n

                    Associate Solution Support Engineer<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                    This role involves providing technical support to SAP customers and troubleshooting complex issues related to SAP products and platforms. Responsibilities include diagnosing problems, performing root cause analysis, utilising knowledge bases, and collaborating with development teams to ensure customer satisfaction and swift issue resolution. The SAP salary for freshers in India ranges from \u20b93.8 lakhs to \u20b96.5 lakhs per year.<\/p>\r\n

                    Sales\/Presales Associate<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                    A Sales\/ Presales Associate supports the sales team by providing technical insights and product knowledge to potential customers. They conduct product demonstrations, assist in identifying customer needs, help prepare proposals, and ensure that proposed SAP solutions align with client requirements, aiding in deal closure. The SAP salary in India for fresher can be between \u20b94 lakhs and \u20b97 lakhs per year for this role. <\/p>\r\n\r\n\r\n

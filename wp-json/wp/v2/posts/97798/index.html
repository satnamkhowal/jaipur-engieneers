Pradhan Mantri Internship Yojana<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

The Prime Minister’s Internship Scheme (PMIS) is a government initiative to provide valuable work experience to young people in India. The scheme offers internships in top companies, aiming to bridge the gap between theory and practical industry experience. The PMIS is part of a pilot project with a budget of Rs 800 crore, and it aims to provide internships to one crore young people over the next five years.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

Key Objectives of the PMIS <\/strong>Internship Program<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n
    \r\n \t
  • To provide young professionals with valuable work experience and exposure to different sectors.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • To enhance the employability of graduates and postgraduates.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • To promote innovation and entrepreneurship among youth.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • To encourage social responsibility and civic engagement.<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

                      PM <\/strong>Government Internship<\/strong> Duration<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                      At least six months of the internship must be spent in a real-world work environment within the internship companies. Classroom training or theoretical learning should not exceed half of the total duration.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                      PM <\/strong>Internship Program<\/strong> Benefits<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                      The selected interns will get a monthly stipend of Rs. 5,000 throughout the 12-month internship program. This stipend is comprised of two parts:<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                        \r\n \t
                      • Government Contribution:<\/strong><\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

                        As this is a government internship, the Gov of India will contribute Rs. 4,500 per month<\/strong> to each intern’s stipend through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to their aadhaar connected bank account.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                          \r\n \t
                        • Company Contribution:\u00a0<\/strong><\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

                          The internship companies will contribute Rs. 500 per month<\/strong> to each intern’s stipend. This contribution is based on the intern’s commitment to the company policies.<\/p>\r\n

                          Internship companies can also provide additional monthly assistance to interns from their funds beyond the mandatory Rs. 500 contribution.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                          PM Internship Eligibility Criteria<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                          To be eligible for the Pradhan Mantri Internship Yojana, applicants must meet the following criteria:<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                            \r\n \t
                          • Citizenship: <\/strong>Applicant should be an Indian National to be eligible for PM Internship.<\/li>\r\n \t
                          • Age: <\/strong>Between 21 and 24 years old (as of the application deadline).<\/li>\r\n \t
                          • Education: <\/strong>Must have completed High School, Higher Secondary School, an ITI certificate, a Polytechnic diploma, or a graduate degree (BA, BSc, BCom, BCA, BBA, BPharma, etc.).<\/li>\r\n \t
                          • Employment: <\/strong>The applicant shouldn\u2019t be currently employed full-time in any organisation. (Those enrolled in online\/distance learning programs are eligible for this Prime Minister Internship Scheme 2024)<\/li>\r\n \t
                          • Family Income: <\/strong>Total family income must be below Rs. 8 lakh annually for the financial year 2023-24. Additionally, no family member should be employed in a full-time government position.<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

                            PM Internship Application<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                            To apply for the PMIS government of india internship, candidates must submit the following documents:<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                              \r\n \t
                            • Aadhaar card<\/li>\r\n \t
                            • Educational certificates<\/li>\r\n \t
                            • Passport-sized photo<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

                              Applicants can only apply for a maximum of five internships at a time.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

                              Government Internship Application Process<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n

                                \r\n \t
                              • Online Registration: <\/strong>Create an account on the official PMIS government internship portal.<\/li>\r\n \t
                              • Profile Creation: <\/strong>Provide accurate information about your personal details, education, work experience, and preferences.<\/li>\r\n \t
                              • Resume Generation: <\/strong>The portal will automatically generate a resume based on the information you provide.<\/li>\r\n \t
                              • Internship Exploration: <\/strong>Browse through available government internships and filter by sector, functional role, location, and other criteria. You can also choose the internship companies.<\/li>\r\n \t
                              • Application Submission: <\/strong>Select up to five government internships that align with your interests and qualifications. You can choose your preferred location (state, district), sector, functional role, and qualifications.<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

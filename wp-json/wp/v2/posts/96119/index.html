    Interview Rounds<\/strong><\/h3>\r\n\r\n

    Below is the Amazon Interview Process and the other aspects like duration and topics involved. There are 5 rounds in the Interview process at Amazon. They are:<\/p>\r\n\r\n

      \r\n \t
    1. Online Written Test<\/li>\r\n \t
    2. Technical Interview Round 1<\/li>\r\n \t
    3. Technical Interview Round 2<\/li>\r\n \t
    4. Hiring Manager Round<\/li>\r\n \t
    5. HR Round<\/li>\r\n<\/ol>\r\n

      Online Written Test<\/strong><\/h4>\r\n

      An online written test serves as the first round of the interview process. The aptitude round, technical round, automata round, and coding round are the four sections that make up this round. This round’s topics include competitive programming, dynamic programming, arrays, matrices, DSA, pointer approach, and strings. This round has an Intermediate difficulty level and lasts for 70 + 30 minutes.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

      Technical Interview Round 1<\/strong><\/h4>\r\n

      Two to three Amazon Software Development Engineers, each with two to five years of experience, will oversee the technical interview. Data structures and algorithms are the foundation for this cycle. There will be two to four questions asked, and 45 to 75 minutes will be allotted. This round’s frequently asked questions center on fundamental ideas in data structures, such as binary search trees, linked lists, binary trees, and matrices.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

      Technical Interview Round 2<\/strong><\/h4>\r\n

      Three managers or software development engineers with five to seven years of experience conduct the interview. Competition or DSA will be the basis for the interview. It will take 45 to 60 minutes, and it will be more challenging than the last round. Additionally, the interviewers will probe the candidate about their projects.<\/p>\r\n

          Hiring Manager Round<\/strong><\/h4>\r\n

          Throughout the entire Amazon Recruitment Process, this round is the most technically demanding. Topics will include design and DS\/Algo challenges, challenging puzzle-based questions, and the candidate’s projects and the technology they utilized to create them.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

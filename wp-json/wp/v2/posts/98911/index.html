About IBM<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

IBM, or International Business Machines Corporation, is a multinational technology company. IBM is a leading provider of hardware, software, and services, offering a wide range of solutions for businesses and organizations of all sizes. Its product portfolio includes mainframe computers, servers, storage systems, networking solutions, middleware, analytics software, cloud computing services, and more.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

What are the C1000 Series Exams?<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

The C1000 series of exams is a set of certification exams provided by IBM. Each exam in this set is designed strategically for specific IBM products, operating platforms, or technologies. These certifications are constructed to validate the candidate\u2019s skills in different IT sectors like cloud computing, AI, cybersecurity, data analytics, etc.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

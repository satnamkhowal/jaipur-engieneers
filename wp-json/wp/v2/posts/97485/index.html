A common term for ethical hacking is “white hat hacking,” which is the deliberate probing of networks, systems, or applications to find security flaws. To help organisations address and resolve these problems before they are exploited, ethical hackers use their expertise to identify potential vulnerabilities that evil hackers might use. This proactive approach helps strengthen cybersecurity defences and protect sensitive data. Ethical hackers operate with permission and follow legal guidelines to ensure their actions are responsible and beneficial.<\/p>\r\n

Each level is designed to build your expertise progressively, ensuring you gain hands-on experience and a deep understanding of ethical hacking.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

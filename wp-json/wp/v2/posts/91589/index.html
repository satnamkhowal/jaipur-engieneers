Unlike “black-hat hackers,” who exploit the infrastructure\u2019s weaknesses for malicious purposes, ethical hackers aim to strengthen security by proactively finding and reporting flaws before malicious actors can exploit them. Ethical Hacking – A Detailed View<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

Ethical hacking is a defensive approach to security. It involves using the exact tools, techniques, and methodologies as malicious attackers, but with the explicit permission of the system owner. The primary goal is to discover security flaws before they can be exploited by criminals. It’s often referred to as “penetration testing” or “pen testing.”<\/p>\r\n\r\n

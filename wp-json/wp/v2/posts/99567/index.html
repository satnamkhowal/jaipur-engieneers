About Accenture<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

Accenture is a global professional software services company providing a broad variety of services, including strategy and consulting, technology, and operations. Accenture helps clients transform their businesses through innovative and digital technologies. Accenture Salary for Freshers<\/a> for various roles ranges from 4L to 8L per year. The salary range grows with your experience and expertise in different job positions.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

Accenture Online Assessment<\/strong><\/h2>\r\n

Accenture’s online assessments are carefully designed to evaluate a candidate’s cognitive abilities, problem-solving skills, and personality traits. These assessments include a variety of methods, such as psychometric tests that gauge numerical, logical, and verbal reasoning. Additionally, Accenture Assessment Test also has situational judgment tests that present realistic work scenarios to assess decision-making skills and the candidates\u2019 alignment with Accenture’s core values. Personality questionnaires further provide information into an individual’s work style and cultural fit within the organisation. Communication assessments in English (grammar, comprehension) may be included for specific roles to evaluate written and verbal communication skills. At the same time, digital interviews allow candidates to showcase their personality and problem-solving abilities in an engaging format. These assessments create a comprehensive evaluation process to identify the best candidates for Accenture.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

Leadership<\/h2>\r\n

Leadership is motivating and influencing people for a selfless cause in most cases. The objectives are usually taken willingly and enthusiastically. They guide people in the right direction by providing inspiration, encouragement and support. Along the journey, leaders check on their followers to make sure they are aligned together. Habitually, they don\u2019t get involved in the making of tactical decisions.<\/p>\r\n

It is more of a social influence process that maximizes the efforts of others toward the achievement of a common goal. It is the result of conformity and requires human resources to achieve the intended outcomes.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

Roles of a Leader<\/h2>\r\n

Most leaders don\u2019t occupy a position in management. It can also be said that a leader does not always need to be in the management team, emphasizing that anyone can be a leader. Following are the core functions of a leader,<\/p>\r\n\r\n

    \r\n \t
  • Coach<\/strong> – <\/strong>The responsibility of a leader is to develop others so that they can succeed in their present tasks and prepare for future ones.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Facilitator<\/strong> – It is necessary for you to make things easy for others. While we usually think of this in the context of a meeting or a group, there are numerous situations when you will be asked to act as a facilitator to help processes flow more smoothly and increase productivity.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Strategist <\/strong>– <\/strong>This position is not limited to an organization’s top leaders. All leaders must think strategically, consider the big picture, and determine which paths and methods the team should take in order to achieve the intended outcomes.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Visionary<\/strong> – Leaders must have a clear sense of where they are going and why that destination is important to the organisation, their team, and themselves.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Change Agent<\/strong> – The need for change is inextricably linked to strategy and vision. As a leader, you must lead and champion the changes you want to see and those that have been invested in you from up the ladder.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Decision Maker <\/strong>– You want others to participate and provide feedback. As a leader, though, you will need to be decisive and make difficult decisions, You must not be scared to make a judgment.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Influencer <\/strong>– This job, whether it’s referred to as mediation, negotiation, or even arbitration, is all about power. As a leader, you must consistently exert a good influence over others.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Team Player<\/strong> – You can’t always be in charge. You must also be a team player, which includes knowing when to relinquish control and allow others to take the lead.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Delegator <\/strong>– You have to accept that you can’t do everything. The finest executives distribute willingly and wisely to ensure that everything gets done and that their workers are prepared to take on larger roles within the company.<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Listener<\/strong> – Leaders frequently believe that they must do all of the talking, but this is a mistake. In reality, the finest leaders inquire, listen, and observe more than they speak. Make sure you pay attention to the people you’re in charge of.<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

    You could have all the leadership qualities at times, but you fail to inspire them because of communication barriers.     Management<\/h2>\r\n

    Managers are hired by the companies to perform with the power of hiring and firing people. They control employers and direct them to work hard and achieve the company\u2019s goal. Management is task-oriented and consists of pre-planned tasks which are to be executed by the subordinates. The primary focus of the management would be meeting the company\u2019s goals and the rest are less often taken into consideration.<\/p>\r\n

    Only the art of management can bring any business forward to success. Good management is necessary as it helps in achieving group goals. Management is to increase the productivity and effectiveness of the employers, processes, projects, and the whole organization itself.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

    Roles of a Manager<\/h2>\r\n

    What managers do? Ever thought of it? A manager is obliged to perform four primary functions. They are planning, organizing, leading, and controlling.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

    They also perform other multiple tasks as listed below,<\/p>\r\n\r\n

      \r\n \t
    • Figurehead <\/strong>– As a manager, you own social, ceremonial, and liable responsibilities. You’re expected to be a source of inspiring light to people.<\/li>\r\n \t
    • Liaison<\/strong> – Managers must communicate with both internal and external stakeholders. You must be able to network effectively on behalf of your company.<\/li>\r\n \t
    • Monitor<\/strong> – In this role, you seek out information about your organization and industry on a regular basis to find the relevant changes in the environment. You also keep track of your team’s productivity as well as their well-being.<\/li>\r\n \t
    • Disseminator<\/strong> – This is where you share potentially useful information with your coworkers and team.<\/li>\r\n \t
    • Spokesperson<\/strong> – Managers represent and speak on behalf of their companies. You are responsible for the communication of information about your company and its goals to people outside of it.<\/li>\r\n \t
    • Entrepreneur<\/strong> – As a manager, you initiate and oversee organizational change. This entails solving problems, coming up with new ideas, and putting them into action.<\/li>\r\n \t
    • Disturbance Handler<\/strong> – When an organization or team encounters an unexpected stumbling block, the manager’s responsibility is to take charge. You must also assist in the resolution of internal conflicts.<\/li>\r\n \t
    • Resource Allocator<\/strong> – You must also decide where organizational resources are best applied. This includes allocating funds as well as assigning personnel and other organizational resources.<\/li>\r\n \t
    • Negotiator<\/strong> – You may be asked to participate in and direct key talks within your team, department, or organization.<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

      Leadership is to let the team fail<\/h2>\r\n

      <\/p>\r\n

      Failure could teach more than what a leader could teach their team. As it is always said, \u2018Failure can be the best teacher in everyone\u2019s life\u2019. Leadership is not all about leading the team to success. It is also about the growth of your team members. Always aiding them in the worst situations and playing the role of a cushion would not let them grow. Great leaders tell their team what is needed and let them do it in their own style. They closely watch them and provide insights into their self-growth. They provide enough space for trial and error for their team.<\/p>\r\n

      A good leader has to train their team members to become leaders of the next generation. The new leaders wouldn\u2019t be strong enough if they don\u2019t face any failures. IBM\u2019s Thomas J Watson often says, \u201cThe fastest way to succeed is to double your failure rate\u201d. The major difference between leader and manager is that most managers fail to do this as they always work for risk controls. Similarly, there are so many unrevealed facts about leadership skills.

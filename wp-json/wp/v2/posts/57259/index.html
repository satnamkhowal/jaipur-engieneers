A job is needed by everyone in this competitive period. A job includes struggle, handling pressure, stress or dealing with difficult people. \u201cStress interview” is one of the ways of examining if the candidate is suitable to cope-up with the stress faced in a job. It enables the employer to notice their dispute management skills at first-hand by employing tactics such as:<\/p>\r\n\r\n

    \r\n \t
  • Unnerving you<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Neglecting you while you are speaking<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Asking unnecessary questions<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Interrupting<\/li>\r\n \t
  • Getting negative personal comments.<\/li>\r\n<\/ul>\r\n

    How can one handle the interview in this kind of stress-full situation? Here are some tips to reduce the stress bomb in an interview.<\/p>\r\n

    Join Spoken English Classes in Bangalore<\/a> at FITA<\/a> which helps in developing your English fluency and overall self-confidence in an interview.<\/p>\r\n\r\n

    \r\n
      \r\n \t
    1. Preparation:<\/strong><\/li>\r\n<\/ol>\r\nIt is necessary to be prepared for the unique and important interaction. You should research and prepare for the company profile, market, product, and competitors before taking up an interview.\r\n\r\nIntrospection is an essential tool. Knowing your weaknesses, strengths, and dealing effectively with them is an important aspect. With essential practices these expected interactions, the tension level will be manageable.\r\n
        \r\n \t
      1. Seclude yourself at the personal level:<\/strong><\/li>\r\n<\/ol>\r\nThe interviewer analyzes your patience by asking irrelevant questions, and they would check whether you wait patiently under pressure or surrender. Take this as a challenge, where you can win if you maintain your self-control despite provocation. Don’t take the negative comments personally.\r\n
          \r\n \t
        1. Body Language:<\/strong><\/li>\r\n<\/ol>\r\nThe interviewer may use different styles and poor body language to provoke you on a defensive side of the inner self. Be calm, breath slowly, limit the speed of your talking and movements. These may help you to be calm. Be aware that the body language and the Eye contact that indicates a positive and enhances confidence.\r\n
            \r\n \t
          1. Take your time and be Confident:<\/strong><\/li>\r\n<\/ol>\r\nThe interviewer may be confusing you and pushing you for an answer. Take time and think more by repeating the question back to make sure you have understood the question well. If you know the answer to those questions reply to them in a polite manner. When you are not aware of the questions, admit that you don\u2019t know.\r\n
              \r\n \t
            1. End Positively:<\/strong><\/li>\r\n<\/ol>\r\nShow your flexibility by maintaining a positive conclusion. e.g. \u201cWell it\u2019s been an exceptionally tough interview, but I know that I can do a great job if given the opportunity\u201d.\r\n\r\n<\/div>\r\n

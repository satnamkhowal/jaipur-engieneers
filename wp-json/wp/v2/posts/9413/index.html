Basics<\/strong><\/p>\r\n

England had an invasion by French-speaking Normans in 1066. As they came in, they brought in a set of French and Latin words to England. England already had Germanic tribes who spoke a kind of Old English. They used words like finde, Hand which are still in usage in German language. The original English language<\/a> known as Anglo-Saxon got its name through the Germanic tribes. The Germanic tribes continued to speak their own version of English. The majority of most common English words has a Germanic origin. Some of the most frequently used words in German and English are from the same roots. The basics in the language of German are almost same with that of English.<\/p>\r\n

Structure and Word Order<\/strong><\/p>\r\n

The languages, English and German also are quite similar in their word order and sentence structure. During the olden times, English had undergone a transition from Middle English to Modern English. Many old Germanic rules were dropped and our modern vocabulary was being created. The adjective, verb or noun could be changed position to insist on its importance like in current day\u2019s German.<\/p>\r\n

Pronunciation<\/strong><\/p>\r\n

Many people feel proud that they have a good amount of command over the language of German because it is considered to be a notoriously hard language. The language has 14 vowels and 18 cases. Words in the language of English and German are fabulously similar in pronunciation. In the past, consonant sounds had minutely changed between English and German. After that, it remained the same. To look into a good example, the German letter P changed to F about 3000 years ago. This resulted in the words like \u2018Ship\u2019 being pronounced as \u2018Schiff\u2019. The umlaut in German clarifies the sounds that we don\u2019t have in the language of English in order to avoid any confusion in the process of pronouncing. As English is not a phonetic, it can sound different than how it is written. But German doesn\u2019t have this problem. This is because of the umlaut.\u00a0 German pronunciation is not that difficult to learn because of its logical structure and similarities with English. Inflection<\/strong><\/p>\r\n

The biggest gap between German and English is mainly caused by those word endings. German is a language that has retained its inflections. This indicates that German words change based on order, tense, gender and number.\u00a0 English is known to have simple inflections as it doesn\u2019t have any strict structure and gender.\u00a0 There is no need of much worry in case of German.<\/p>\r\n

Outside Influences<\/strong><\/p>\r\n

The languages have had a lot of outside influences. Normans, Germanics had a great role in influencing the language of English. Many Celtic, Norse and Latin words have been moved between German and English.<\/p>\r\n
